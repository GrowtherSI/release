# Referrals and discount codes

Growther.si has two referral programmes. Both live on your account at
[license.growther.si](https://license.growther.si/account), not inside C5, and both
are described in full in the [Terms of Service](https://license.growther.si/terms)
under **Referrals**.

| | Free-days referral | Special referral link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who shares it | Anyone with an account | A referrer we have validated |
| What it looks like | A code and a link on your Account page | `growther.si/<name>` |
| What the buyer gets | Free days added when they make their first purchase | A discount at checkout — a percentage or an amount off, for a set number of months on recurring plans (some offers add free days too) |
| What the sharer gets | The same free days, when the referred purchase clears | A share of what their buyers pay, through Stripe |
| Can they combine? | Yes — a special discount and a free-days referral can be used in the same checkout | |

## Free-days referral

1. Open your [Account page](https://license.growther.si/account) and click
   **Create referral code** under *Refer a friend*. You get a code, a link and a QR
   code.
2. A friend opens the link, signs in and redeems the code. Nothing is granted yet.
3. When they make their first paid purchase, both of you receive the free days.
   They are added to your day balance and appear in *Transactions*.

One code per account can be redeemed, you cannot redeem your own, and the days
have no cash value. If a promotion set the number of days when the code was
redeemed, the purchase has to happen before that promotion ends.

## Using a special referral link

Someone you know may send you a link of the form `growther.si/<name>`. Opening it
takes you to the licence portal, where a banner shows who sent it and what it is
worth, for example *"jane sent you 20% off C5 licences and Mothership — on recurring
plans for the first 6 months"*.

- **Sign in to keep it.** We hold the offer for 30 days from opening the link. It
  is attached to your account as soon as you sign in, and the cart on the Plans
  page shows what comes off, line by line. Stripe's checkout page shows the same figure — except a per-user line (extra users, Mothership), which Stripe prices, so that part of the discount shows only on Stripe's page; the cart marks the total with a + when that is the case.
- **Have a code but no link?** On the Plans page, open *Have a referral code?* in the
  cart summary and enter it.
- **Already a subscriber?** If your subscription is one the offer covers, the *Your
  discount* card on your Account page shows *Apply to my subscription*. The
  discount starts with your next invoice; nothing already paid changes.
- **Time limit.** Use the offer within 30 days of opening the link, or by the
  link's own end date if that comes first. If you start a free trial in that time,
  the window extends to 30 days after the trial ends — but never past the link's own end date.
- **One at a time.** An account holds one special offer at a time and it applies
  once, to your first qualifying purchase after it attached. It never applies to
  payments you have already made, and it never combines with another special
  offer — but it does combine with the free-days referral above.
- **Cart has nothing it covers?** The offer stays attached for a later purchase.
  The same happens when a checkout uses your trade-in credit instead.
- **If it is no longer available** — the link was paused, filled up or expired — the
  cart says so and checkout runs at the regular price.
- **Left the checkout page?** A checkout you start holds ("reserves") the offer.
  If you closed Stripe's page without paying, the cart says so and offers *Release
  and use it here*, which puts the discount back on your next attempt. (If that checkout left you an invoice that is still due, the offer stays on the invoice until you pay it or it lapses.)

The link sets one strictly necessary cookie (`gw_ref`, 30 days) on
`license.growther.si` that carries only the code. The person who sent the link
never learns who you are; they see only how many people opened it and bought.

## Becoming a referrer

If you would like a personal link that pays you a share of what your buyers spend:

1. Open your [Account page](https://license.growther.si/account) → **Referral
   Program** → *Apply to the Referral Program*.
2. Give your name (kept private), a public handle (what buyers see), the email we
   should use, your country, and the link you would like — `growther.si/your-name` —
   or let us generate one. The form checks live whether a name is free.
3. Your application shows as **Pending**. We review every application and email
   you the current rates. Once terms are agreed we validate it, email you, and
   your Account page shows the link, a QR code, what your buyers get, and a
   *Stats* button with your counts: link opens, offers waiting, first purchases,
   paid cycles. Counts only — never a buyer's name or email.

**How you are paid.** Payouts are made only through Stripe, in one of two ways:

- **By email (United States only).** Tick *I confirm I am in the United States* on
  your application and we send a Stripe payout to your email address; Stripe asks
  for your bank details the first time.
- **Your own Stripe account (anywhere Stripe supports).** Click *Set up payouts
  with Stripe* on your Account page — it takes a few minutes — and your share is
  transferred automatically. Outside the United States this is required before a
  paying link can go live. You can switch to this at any time; anything already
  earned moves over. *Open Stripe* takes you to your Stripe dashboard afterwards.

We never pay by cheque or by bank transfer outside Stripe.

Your share of each qualifying payment becomes payable thirty days after the payment
clears, and is reversed if that payment is refunded or disputed in that time. Rates
are set when your application is validated and may change if you re-apply.

**Changing your mind.** *Edit* lets you change your details; this returns the
application to review, and the link keeps working for buyers who already have it.
Once the link is live, its address is fixed — contact us to change it. *Withdraw*
cancels a pending application. *Deactivate* switches a live link off: new sign-ups
through it stop at once, discounts already given keep running, and money already
earned is still paid as usual. *Apply again* whenever you like.

## Questions

**I opened a link but see no banner.** Either the link is not active right now (the page tells you so) or you are on a different browser or device from the one that opened it. Sign in and enter the code under *Have a referral code?* in the cart instead.

**The banner says the offer could not be attached.** Your account already holds a
special offer, or the link is your own. One special offer per account. It can also mean the offer was paused, filled up or expired after you opened the link — the banner says which.

**My discount is missing from Stripe's checkout page.** Go back to the Plans page:
if the cart shows the offer as no longer available, it was paused, filled up or
expired after you attached it, and checkout runs at the regular price.

**As a referrer, where do I see money?** In Stripe. The Account page shows counts
only; the payout itself arrives from Stripe, by email or into the Stripe account
you set up.
